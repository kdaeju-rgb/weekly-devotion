Acts 17:1–14 5-Day Devotional

Is This
Really
So?

Thessalonica and Berea heard the same gospel and answered in two very different ways. Walk five days through Acts 17 and discover the posture faith calls us to.

01 A Settled Practice. Acts 17:2
02 The Face of Envy. Acts 17:5
03 Examined Daily. Acts 17:11
04 Another King. Acts 17:7
05 A New King's People. Acts 17:14
01
DAY 01 A Settled Practice Monday

The gospel is not emotion —
it is Scripture, reasoned and proclaimed.

"and Paul went in, as was his custom, and on three Sabbath days he reasoned with them from the Scriptures."

Acts 17:2 (ESV)

? Do you have a settled practice of standing on Scripture rather than leaning on emotion alone? If not, where could you begin today?

— Lord, let me stand on your word, not just my feelings.

02
DAY 02 The Face of Envy Tuesday

Envy will make us
abandon our own principles.

"But the Jews were jealous, and taking some wicked men of the rabble, they formed a mob, set the city in an uproar, and attacked the house of Jason."

Acts 17:5 (ESV)

? When your influence feels threatened, whose company do you find yourself keeping — people you would normally never align with?

— Guard my principles even when I feel threatened.

03
DAY 03 Examined Daily Wednesday

Eager to receive,
yet daily testing it against Scripture — this is maturity.

"Now these Jews were more noble than those in Thessalonica; they received the word with all eagerness, examining the Scriptures daily to see if these things were so."

Acts 17:11 (ESV)

? What teaching have you accepted lately without ever testing it against Scripture?

— Give me a heart eager to receive and honest enough to examine.

04
DAY 04 Another King Thursday

The moment we crown Jesus king,
every other throne loses its claim on us.

"and Jason has received them, and they are all acting against the decrees of Caesar, saying that there is another king, Jesus."

Acts 17:7 (ESV)

? What rules your life the way Caesar once ruled Thessalonica? Is Jesus truly king over that place?

— Let me proclaim the true King, even when it costs me.

05
DAY 05 A New King's People Friday

Loyal without reward,
faithful even without an answer — that is our strength.

"Then the brothers immediately sent Paul off on his way to the sea, but Silas and Timothy remained there."

Acts 17:14 (ESV)

? What is one concrete way you will trust the kingship of Jesus Christ this week?

— Let me trust your reign, starting here, starting today.

This Week in Three Words

Eager · Examine · Bold

Like the Bereans — the three postures we are called to live out before God's word.

Closing Prayer

Lord,
when truth confronts us, keep us from envy and fear.
Give us a mature faith — eager to receive,
and honest enough to examine daily.

— A M E N .
