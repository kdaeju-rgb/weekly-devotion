Day 01 · The Lines We Draw

We draw lines
by instinct.

When word came that even Gentiles had received the Word, the church's first response was criticism.

"So when Peter went up to Jerusalem, the circumcision party criticized him, saying, 'You went to uncircumcised men and ate with them.'"

Acts 11:2-3 · ESV

Day 01 · Meditation

Before the good news of salvation,
what caught them was "you ate with them."

From birth until today, we have lived to confirm that we are different from others. Our neighborhood, our degrees, our clothes, our connections — even the church we attend — all become ways of ranking ourselves above someone else.

The believers in Jerusalem knew, in their heads, that Jew and Gentile were one in the gospel. But hearts trained by a lifetime of drawing lines were still not ready to receive a kingdom without walls.

Day 01 · Application + Prayer

Look honestly at the lines inside me.

01 Neighborhood, degrees, clothes, connections — what am I using to draw a line between myself and others?
02 Even inside the church, am I quietly sorting people into insiders and outsiders?

Father, show me first how comfortable my heart has become with drawing lines.
Keep me from weighing qualifications before grace.

— Amen.
Day 02 · What God Has Made Clean

Do not call it
common.

Before the sheet lowered from heaven, the standard Peter had kept his whole life began to shake.

"But the voice answered a second time from heaven, 'What God has made clean, do not call common.'"

Acts 11:9 · ESV

Day 02 · Meditation

A standard built out of zeal
can become a weapon that divides people.

There is not a single line in the law that says touching a Gentile makes you unclean. The rule began as zeal for holiness and purity, but somewhere along the way it became a pretext — a means of creating an uncrossable gap between Jew and Gentile.

That is why we must not be deceived by our own diligence. When we dress up our plausible motives and good intentions as the will of God, we may lose as much as we keep.

Day 02 · Application + Prayer

Examine what lies beneath my zeal.

01 What standard of mine looks like zeal for holiness, but in truth exists to set me apart from others?
02 Have I ever dressed up my own good intentions as the will of God?

Lord, keep me from being deceived by my own diligence.
Keep me from dividing people with well-dressed motives.

— Amen.
Day 03 · The Same Spirit, the Same Gift

The Spirit was
never ours alone.

Before the sermon was even finished, the Spirit fell on them too.

"As I began to speak, the Holy Spirit fell on them just as on us at the beginning."

Acts 11:15 · ESV

Day 03 · Meditation

God was already present in the very place
people had pushed away.

The Jews locked the doors so the Gentiles could not come in. But God went himself — into the house of Cornelius the Gentile — and poured out the Spirit exactly as he had in the upper room.

Just as the gospel was never Jewish property, the Spirit was never a Jews-only grace. People stood blocking the door, saying "never" — and God said, "I have already received them."

Day 03 · Application + Prayer

Revisit the place I pushed someone away.

01 Who is the person I have pushed out of my heart, saying "not that one"?
02 If God was already there ahead of me, what do I need to lay down?

Lord, keep me from measuring your grace by my own standards.
Let me follow with joy wherever you go.

— Amen.
Day 04 · Who Was I?

Who am I to stand
in God's way?

This is not mere humility. It is surrender to the Word — a confession of repentance.

"If then God gave the same gift to them as he gave to us when we believed in the Lord Jesus Christ, who was I that I could stand in God's way?"

Acts 11:17 · ESV

Day 04 · Meditation

Our best is not doing something for God,
but making room for God.

Through the salvation of Cornelius's household, God melted away the prejudice, the false assumptions, and the stubbornness that had long sat enthroned in Peter. The self-assured old Peter was no longer there.

The best thing we can do is not to accomplish something for the Lord, but to give him room to work freely within us — and to stop deciding for him how he ought to use us.

Day 04 · Application + Prayer

Stand before the wall that must fall.

01 Am I pushing away someone God has received? Am I judging someone God has embraced?
02 My pride, my experience, my sense of spiritual superiority — which old wall of mine must fall before the Word?

Lord, I saw it wrongly. I judged people by my own standards
and ran ahead of you. I will no longer stand in your way.

— Amen.
Day 05 · They Glorified God

Father, you
have done well.

The critics fell silent — and immediately gave glory to God.

"When they heard these things they fell silent. And they glorified God, saying, 'Then to the Gentiles also God has granted repentance that leads to life.'"

Acts 11:18 · ESV

Day 05 · Meditation

Not a grudging admission,
but a confession of joy.

It was not, "Well, if God is doing it, what can we say." It was, "Father, you have done well. What you have done is right." They gave thanks and glory to the God who had granted life-giving repentance even to the Gentiles.

And the gospel that had been confined to "me," "my side," "my church" burst out like floodwater toward all the earth. The church is not a place that sorts people out, but a place that embraces everyone God has brought to life.

Closing Prayer

Carry one thing
out of this week.

01 Who is the one person I will welcome with joy this week?
02 Which sentence from this week's meditations stayed with you most — and how will you live it out?

Father, you have done well. It is right that you gave grace to them too.
Let me welcome with joy the ones you have already welcomed.

— Amen.
