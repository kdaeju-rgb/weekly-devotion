Small Group Discussion Sheet

The Gospel

Friday Young Adults Gathering · November 6, 2026
Text Romans 1:2–7 (ESV) · Reference Genesis 15:17; Matthew 1:21–23; 2 Corinthians 4:7 (ESV)
1Icebreaker

Have you ever waited eagerly for some news, thinking, “If only this happens, I’ll be truly happy” (an acceptance letter, a job offer, a birthday gift, a new phone)? Once it actually happened, how long did the joy last?

2Message Summary

In a word, the gospel is Jesus Christ

When Paul says he was “set apart for the gospel,” he does not mean that he was called after he had qualified himself by becoming a servant and a “small one.” It was the other way around. God first set Paul apart while he was still in his sin, and by that grace Paul was able to become small and become the Lord’s servant. We were not saved because we passed God’s cutoff line, but because God compelled us by His grace. When God made His covenant with Abraham, He alone passed between the pieces of the sacrifice. This meant that God alone would begin the covenant, and God alone would fulfill it. The One who could raise up children for Abraham from stones calls us and loves us as if He could not do without us.

The word “gospel” (euangelion) was originally used in Rome to celebrate the birth of Emperor Augustus. But the good news of an empire that would fall within a few centuries could never be the real gospel. Money, health, a job, or an acceptance letter cannot keep us happy for more than a week, because what is really killing us is not poverty or failure but sin. Jesus is the gospel for two reasons. First, He is the only One who saves us from our sins while we stand under a death sentence (Jesus). Second, by restoring our broken relationship with God, He means that God is with us (Immanuel). When God is with us, even a hellish reality becomes the kingdom of heaven. The God who once passed alone between the pieces was finally torn apart for us on the cross. The greatest treasure, the Lord Himself, has been placed in ordinary jars of clay like us. That is the gospel.

“But we have this treasure in jars of clay, to show that the surpassing power belongs to God and not to us.”
2 Corinthians 4:7
3Observation Questions
  1. “Set apart” means to be separated and appointed. If Paul was not set apart because he had become a servant and a small one, what is the real order of things? Romans 1:1; Galatians 1:15
  2. In ancient Near Eastern covenant rituals, what did it mean to walk between the halves of a sacrifice?
    Why do you think God passed through alone instead of with Abraham? Genesis 15:9–17
  3. Matthew treats “Jesus” (He will save His people from their sins) and “Immanuel” (God with us) as meaning the same thing. How are these two names connected? Matthew 1:21–23; Romans 1:3–4
4Life Application
  1. For Paul, his background, learning, and righteousness under the law were a “fake gospel.” What fake gospel are you holding onto right now, thinking, “If I just have this, I can live”?
  2. What would a delicious meal or a winning lottery ticket mean to someone on death row? In light of this illustration, is Jesus truly “good news” to you right now? Share honestly.
  3. When do you feel like a “worthless jar of clay”? If you remembered that the Lord Himself dwells in that jar, how might you see your situation or relationships differently this week?
5Prayer Together
  • That we would remember every day, with gratitude, the sheer grace of God who chose and called us before we were worthy
  • That we would let go of fake gospels like acceptance letters, jobs, money, and approval, and hold onto Jesus Christ alone as our gospel
  • That our restless hearts would find rest in the Lord, and that lonely, hellish realities would be turned into the kingdom of heaven with Him
  • That our jar-of-clay lives would reveal the treasure within, and that the change that happened to Paul would happen to us as well
6Closing Prayer

Loving Father, thank You for choosing and calling us first, though we were neither Your servants nor small before You. Just as You passed alone between the pieces of the sacrifice, we believe that You alone began our salvation and You alone will complete it. Lord, for so long we have clung to fake gospels. We believed we would be happy if only we got in, got the job, had the money, or won someone’s approval, yet that joy did not last even a week. Have mercy on us. We trust in Jesus, who rescued us from sin while we stood under a death sentence, and who will lead us beyond the grave. Let our restless hearts now find rest in You, and turn our hellish realities into the kingdom of heaven where You are with us. You have placed Yourself, our greatest treasure, in these ordinary jars of clay; may our lives reveal that treasure. In the name of Jesus Christ, who has become the true gospel for us, we pray.

Amen.
