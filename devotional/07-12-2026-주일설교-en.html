DAY 01 · The Battle of Standards

What sets
the standard
for your life?

When someone else becomes our standard, every outcome ends in misery.

"And the hand of the Lord was with them, and a great number who believed turned to the Lord."

Acts 11:21 · ESV

DAY 01 · Meditation

When a dog becomes
the standard, every verdict
is misery.

A man once raced a dog and lost. A bystander said, "You're worse than a dog." He trained and tied the dog next time — "Now you're just like a dog." He trained harder still and beat the dog — "Worse yet, you had to race a dog to feel good."

The same happens when another person becomes our standard. A tree does not envy the birds singing in its branches, and a summer flower does not envy the blossoms of spring. The standard for our lives must always be the mission God himself has entrusted to us.

DAY 01 · Reflect + Pray

Let go of comparison for one day this week.

01 Who have you quietly been comparing yourself to lately?
02 How has that comparison dimmed the joy of the calling God has given you?

Lord,
Let the standard of my life be not people,
but the mission you have entrusted to me.

— Amen.
DAY 02 · Grace Alone

Not worthiness —
only grace
was seen.

Barnabas never asked whether the grace received was Jewish or Gentile.

"When he came and saw the grace of God, he was glad, and he exhorted them all to remain faithful to the Lord with steadfast purpose."

Acts 11:23 · ESV

DAY 02 · Meditation

He did not weigh who deserved it —
he simply rejoiced to see grace.

The Jerusalem church sent Barnabas to Antioch to verify the rumor that Gentiles, too, were being saved. When he arrived, he did not ask whether the recipients of grace were Jews or Gentiles. He simply saw the grace of God being poured out, and rejoiced.

Barnabas performed no recorded miracles or exorcisms, yet he was a good man, full of the Holy Spirit and of faith, and a great many people were added to the Lord. Years later he sought out Paul, forgotten for over a decade, installed him as co-pastor of Antioch, and quietly stepped back himself.

DAY 02 · Reflect + Pray

Simply rejoice today over someone else's grace.

01 Have you ever caught yourself weighing whether someone deserved the grace they received?
02 Do you have the courage to step back from something you built with your own hands?

Lord,
Help me see grace without weighing worthiness.
Remind me that even what I have built belongs to you.

— Amen.
DAY 03 · Earn the Name

Christian is a name
you must earn,
not claim.

A Hellenistic Jew, a former slave, a nameless Gentile, a man of Rome's ruling class, and a former enemy of the church — the Antioch church was one in Christ.

"...and in Antioch the disciples were first called Christians."

Acts 11:26 · ESV

DAY 03 · Meditation

Saying "I am a Christian"
on your own means nothing.

The leaders of the Antioch church were Barnabas (an orthodox Jew), Simeon called Niger (a former Black slave), Lucius of Cyrene (an unnamed Gentile), Manaen (a member of Rome's ruling class), and Saul (once the church's fiercest enemy). Utterly different backgrounds, made one in Christ.

The disciples were called "Christians" not because they named themselves that, but because the people of Antioch recognized something in them and gave them the name — because they resembled the life and way of Christ.

DAY 03 · Reflect + Pray

Be called "Christian" by someone today — not by your own claim.

01 Could your own family and neighbors look at you and call you a Christian?
02 Can you be one in Christ with someone whose background is nothing like yours?

Lord,
Let my life reveal Christ,
even when I say nothing about myself.

— Amen.
DAY 04 · Live the Message

The gospel is
preached — and
watched at once.

People notice the messenger more closely than the message.

"...for he was a good man, full of the Holy Spirit and of faith. And a great many people were added to the Lord."

Acts 11:24 · ESV

DAY 04 · Meditation

A dirty delivery truck made
people distrust the milk inside.

A Chinese dairy company once ran a hugely successful ad campaign with a fleet of one hundred delivery trucks. Sales soared — until the trucks grew dirty and no one thought to wash them. Sales suddenly collapsed. Seeing the filthy trucks, customers assumed the milk inside was unclean too.

The gospel works the same way. The early church left no record of evangelistic festivals, yet the very lives of believers became a living textbook, and people came to faith on their own. The church itself was the message, the gospel, and the mission all at once.

DAY 04 · Reflect + Pray

Examine what message your life is preaching today.

01 What do the people watching your life actually see through you?
02 Where does the gap show between what you say and how you live?

Lord,
Let my life preach the gospel
before my words ever do.

— Amen.
DAY 05 · Treasure in Clay

A weak and few
can still
change the world.

As three percent salinity makes the whole sea salty, a devoted few can change the world.

"But we have this treasure in jars of clay, to show that the surpassing power belongs to God and not to us."

2 Corinthians 4:7 · ESV

DAY 05 · Meditation

Paper taped to a wall doesn't tear —
not because the paper is strong.

The early church's power was never in its size or its buildings, but in a life so distinct from the world that it drew people in. They possessed nothing, yet carried a peace as though they possessed everything — even a Paul imprisoned in chains could tell those outside to rejoice.

The treasure of Jesus Christ is carried in fragile jars of clay, and it is that treasure alone, fully revealed, that becomes the secret of a church where "a great many are added to the Lord."

Closing Prayer

Live this week by one mission alone, without comparison.

01 What is the one mission you will live out this week without comparing yourself to anyone?
02 How will you practice today the truth that it is Christ, not you, who should be seen in your life?

Lord,
Give me the faith and courage to let go of what does not matter.
Make me full of your Spirit and faith, that a great many may be added to you.

— Amen.
