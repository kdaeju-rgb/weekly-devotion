Small Group Discussion Sheet

From Luz to Bethel

Friday Young Adults Gathering · September 25, 2026
Text Genesis 28:16–19 (ESV) · Reference Genesis 28:12–15 (ESV)
1Icebreaker

Have you ever felt alone and lost in an unfamiliar place — your first day abroad, a move, a time you got lost? What was the first thing you reached for?

2Message Summary

Where a hiding place becomes God's house

Jacob took his brother's birthright and stole his blessing by deception — and ended up losing everything, fleeing more than 500 miles from home. It was a wilderness of his own making, yet God met him right there and showed him a ladder reaching to heaven. The ladder is a tool of grace: it makes us realize, "I can never reach God by my own strength." In Hebrew, wilderness (midbar) shares its root with word (davar) and the Most Holy Place (dvir). The wilderness, where there is nothing else to lean on, is where we see God most clearly.

Waking from his sleep, Jacob confessed, "Surely the LORD is in this place, and I did not know it," and he renamed Luz — "a place of refuge" — Bethel, "the house of God." God had not suddenly appeared; He had been there all along. Jacob's circumstances didn't change overnight, but the God of his grandfather and father became Jacob's own God. God doesn't show us the road 100 meters ahead; He guides the step right in front of us. When we trust Him and obey today, our Luz becomes Bethel.

"Then Jacob awoke from his sleep and said, 'Surely the LORD is in this place, and I did not know it.'"
Genesis 28:16 · ESV
3Observation Questions
  1. Why did Jacob have to leave home and flee? Whose wrongdoing led him into this wilderness? Genesis 27–28
  2. What did the ladder in Jacob's dream help him realize? What promise did God give him there? Genesis 28:12–15
  3. What does it mean that Jacob renamed the place from Luz to Bethel? Genesis 28:19
4Life Application
  1. Like the traveler in Tolstoy's parable, what "drops of honey" am I licking even while hanging between life and death?
  2. Where is my "wilderness" or "Luz" right now? Have you ever discovered that God was with you there? Share that moment.
  3. Not ten years from now, but in today's single step — what is God asking me to obey? Choose one concrete thing to practice this week.
5Prayer Together
  • For forgiveness for the times we forgot God, intoxicated by the sweetness of the world
  • For those walking through a wilderness now, that they would meet the God who is with them there
  • For a faith that confesses "my God," not only my parents' God
  • For faith to obey today's one step, even when the road ahead is unclear
6Closing Prayer

Loving God, we thank you. Just as Jacob met you when he had lost everything and collapsed alone in the wilderness, restore in us the confession of faith, "Surely the LORD was with me in this place," even in the wilderness places of our lives. Thank you for calling us back to yourself tonight — we who are worn out from trying to live by our own strength and wisdom. We long for the Luz we fled to in shame to be transformed, in your hands, into Bethel, the house of God. Even when you do not show us the road 100 meters ahead, help us trust you to hold the one step we take today, and let those steps of faith add up until we arrive at the place you have prepared. Forgive us for forgetting you, caught between life and death and drunk on the sweetness of this world, and from now on, wherever we are, help us live believing that where you are is the kingdom of heaven. We pray in the name of Jesus.

Amen.
