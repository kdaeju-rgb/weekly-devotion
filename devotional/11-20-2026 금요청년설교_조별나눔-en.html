Small Group Discussion Sheet

By Faith Alone

Friday Young Adults Gathering · November 20, 2026
Text Romans 1:16–17 (ESV) · Reference John 20:25; 12:45 (ESV)
1Icebreaker

Have you ever flown a kite? Or do you remember finding your way somewhere new without GPS, trusting only someone’s directions or a hand-drawn map?

2Message Summary

There is only one string. God alone is our string.

People say, “Show me God, and then I’ll believe.” But finite people can’t contain an infinite God with reason. As Augustine said, “If you comprehend Him, He is not God.” The Bible doesn’t say we reach faith through understanding; it says God’s righteousness is revealed “from faith for faith.” Like a hand-drawn map before GPS, you have to trust it and go before you understand it. But the bigger problem today is that so few Christians draw an accurate map. The world can’t see the invisible God; it can only see the lives of those who say they believe in Him. In a Roman world full of gods, the early Christians were called atheists and faced social isolation, yet the distinct beauty of their lives drew people to Jesus.

Paul was not ashamed of the gospel, even though it looked weak and shameful in the eyes of the world. Knowledge, degrees, fame, even the power of Caesar cannot give salvation; only Jesus Christ can. At Han River Park, a kite soaring high in the sky nosedived into the ground the moment its string snapped. For a kite, the string is its life. We hold on to money, youth, careers, degrees, and connections as our string, and when our plans fall apart, we feel like “a kite with a broken string.” But those strings didn’t break; they were never the string in the first place. Sadly, we usually realize our string is fake only when it snaps, and so in His mercy and love, God sometimes allows us to hurt. Because the almighty God loves us, nothing happens by accident. There is only one string, and God alone, the string that never breaks, is our string.

“For in it the righteousness of God is revealed from faith for faith, as it is written, ‘The righteous shall live by faith.’”
Romans 1:17
3Observation Questions
  1. What does “from faith for faith” tell us about the order of understanding and faith? How would you explain it using the hand-drawn map illustration? Romans 1:17; John 20:25
  2. Jesus said, “Whoever sees me sees him who sent me.” In a Roman world full of gods, how did the early Christians show God through their lives? John 12:45
  3. Why did the gospel look shameful to people at the time? And why was Paul still not ashamed of it? Romans 1:16
4Life Application
  1. If you finished the sentence, “I live by ___ alone,” honestly, what string are you holding on to right now? (Money, grades, résumé, degrees, connections, relationships…)
  2. Have you ever felt like “a kite with a broken string” in your job search, career path, or relationships? Looking back, how did you discover God’s mercy in that pain?
  3. Friends and family who don’t believe watch our lives more than they listen to our words. What concrete step could you take this week so that your life becomes a “map” that points to God?
5Prayer Together
  • That we would let go of fake strings like money, résumés, degrees, and connections, and live with God alone, the string that never breaks
  • That even in things we can’t understand, we would believe nothing happens by accident and keep walking in trust of God’s faithfulness
  • That we would become Christians with a distinct beauty, showing Jesus Christ not only with our words but with our lives
  • For those in our community waiting or discouraged over jobs, careers, exams, or relationships, that they would experience the Lord’s mercy
6Closing Prayer

Loving Father God, forgive us for insisting that we must understand before we believe, and for trusting the visible world more than You, the invisible God. We have held on to money and youth, careers and degrees and connections as our string, and whenever they were shaken, we sank down like a kite with a broken string. Help us see that they were never the string in the first place, and hold on only to You, the string that never breaks. When jobs and marriage are delayed, when we fail exams, when our plans fall apart, help us lean on Your mercy, which is teaching us to let go of fake strings. In the face of things we can’t understand, help us believe that because the almighty God loves us, nothing happens by accident. And even when the world mocks it as weak, let us boldly confess that Jesus Christ alone is the power of God for salvation. Right now, we choose to lay down the fake strings clutched in our hands and to live with You alone as our string. Let this confession not end as tonight’s resolution, but become our everyday life. Holding on to Your word, “The righteous shall live by faith,” we pray in the name of Jesus Christ.

Amen.
