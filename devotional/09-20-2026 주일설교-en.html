Sunday Sermon · 09.20.2026 5-Day Devotional

You received
the Spirit
when you believed.

Assurance of salvation is not a feeling or an experience — it rests on the finished, certain promise of the cross of Jesus Christ. This week, let's walk together as children of God who have already received the Spirit.

01 Baptized with the Spirit and fire. Matt 3:11
02 The Spirit received when you believed. Acts 19:2
03 Faith and Spirit — one moment. Acts 19:4
04 A tree known by its fruit. Matt 7:16
05 The fruit of patience. Gal 5:22
01
DAY 01 Baptized with the Spirit and Fire Monday

Assurance of salvation is not
looking at yourself — it's looking at Christ.

"I baptize you with water for repentance, but he who is coming after me is mightier than I... He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and fire."

Matthew 3:11 · ESV

? Have I been searching for assurance of salvation in my own feelings and experiences?

— Lord, let me hold on to your promise, not my feelings.

02
DAY 02 The Spirit Received in Believing Tuesday

Knowing something in your head,
and meeting it in your life — these are not the same.

"Did you receive the Holy Spirit when you believed? ... No, we have not even heard that there is a Holy Spirit."

Acts 19:2 · ESV

? Do I only know the Spirit in my head, without yet meeting him in daily life?

— Lord, let me live out today what I already know.

03
DAY 03 Faith and Spirit, One Moment Wednesday

We don't pray to be saved —
we pray because we already are.

"John baptized with the baptism of repentance, telling the people to believe in the one who was to come after him, that is, Jesus."

Acts 19:4 · ESV

? Am I striving to earn my identity, or praying because I want to live worthy of the identity I already have?

— Lord, let me trust the identity you have already given me.

04
DAY 04 A Tree Known by Its Fruit Thursday

The one place we cannot hide our faith
is our own home.

"You will recognize them by their fruits... every healthy tree bears good fruit, but the diseased tree bears bad fruit."

Matthew 7:16-18 · ESV

? What fruit does my own family see when they look at my faith?

— Lord, let the fruit of the Spirit begin at home.

05
DAY 05 The Fruit of Patience Friday

Even on a day that feels unchanged,
patient endurance is fruit of the Spirit.

"But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, self-control; against such things there is no law."

Galatians 5:22-23 · ESV

? Where, this week, do I need to keep enduring patiently to the end?

— Lord, let me follow you with a patience that does not grow weary.

This Week's Conclusion

Faith · Identity · Fruit

Three postures required of those who received the Spirit when they believed.

Closing Prayer

Lord,
beyond feeling and emotion, let me hold on to the grace and promise of your cross.
Today again, let me bear the fruit of the Spirit with patience.

— A M E N .
