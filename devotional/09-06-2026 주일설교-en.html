Acts 17 5-Day Devotional

The Information Age,
The Land of Idols.

Athens was a city of the intellect — and a city full of idols. Information overflows today, yet information cannot save. Follow Paul's sermon at the Areopagus through five days of meditation this week.

01 A City Full of Idols. Acts 17:16
02 The Unvarnished Gospel. Acts 17:18
03 Truth as Content. Acts 17:21
04 A God We Cannot Make. Acts 17:24-25
05 One Changed Life. Acts 17:34
01
DAY 01 Athens, Our City Today Monday

Knowledge has grown —
but idols have only grown more sophisticated.

"Now while Paul was waiting for them at Athens, his spirit was provoked within him as he saw that the city was full of idols."

Acts 17:16 · ESV

? Success, recognition, credentials, image, follower counts — what new idol has quietly taken shape in your life?

— Open my eyes to discern the idols hidden in abundant information.

02
DAY 02 The Unvarnished Gospel Tuesday

The gospel is not a message shaped to please people —
it is a witness to what God has already done.

"Some of the Epicurean and Stoic philosophers also conversed with him. And some said, 'What does this babbler wish to say?' Others said, 'He seems to be a preacher of foreign divinities' — because he was preaching Jesus and the resurrection."

Acts 17:18 · ESV

? Have you ever softened or repackaged the gospel because you feared how people would react?

— Let me proclaim Jesus and the resurrection plainly, even in the face of mockery.

03
DAY 03 Truth Consumed as Content Wednesday

We consume whatever is newest —
but we do not bow before the truth.

"Now all the Athenians and the foreigners who lived there would spend their time in nothing except telling or hearing something new."

Acts 17:21 · ESV

? Where have you merely consumed God's word like content, without letting it lead you to obedience?

— Make me not merely a hearer, but a doer of your word.

04
DAY 04 A God We Cannot Fabricate Thursday

God cannot be shaped
into whatever we find useful.

"The God who made the world and everything in it, being Lord of heaven and earth, does not live in temples made by man, nor is he served by human hands, as though he needed anything, since he himself gives to all mankind life and breath and everything."

Acts 17:24-25 · ESV

? Is there a "manageable god" you serve — one you quietly expect to move on your own terms?

— Let me worship the God who made me, not a god I have made.

05
DAY 05 One Changed Life Friday

Not information, but the cross and resurrection —
this is what shakes us to the core.

"But some men joined him and believed, among whom also were Dionysius the Areopagite and a woman named Damaris and others with them."

Acts 17:34 · ESV

? This week, what would it look like for your life to bear witness as "one changed person"?

— Let my own heart be shaken first, at the foot of the cross and the empty tomb.

This Week's Conclusion

Discern · Speak Plainly · Worship

Three postures that keep us steady in a flood of information.

Closing Prayer

Father, we see that information does not make idols disappear.
Let only Jesus Christ, crucified and risen,
be the power that shakes even the most learned, most cynical heart —
starting with our own.

— A M E N .
