Sunday Sermon · 09.27.2026 5-Day Devotional

Frenzy
without
courage.

"Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!" — two hours of shouting, and fear hidden underneath.
This week, let's ask together whether the string we are holding is truly Jesus.

01
DAY 01 The King Has Changed Monday

When the gospel arrives,
the world is bound to shake.

"About that time there arose no little disturbance concerning the Way."

Acts 19:23 · ESV

? Where does living under King Jesus put me at odds with the way the world works?
If there is no friction at all, why might that be?

— Lord, let the way I live show that you are my King.

02
DAY 02 It Was About Money Tuesday

They claimed to defend a goddess,
but they were defending their wallets.

"And there is danger not only that this trade of ours may come into disrepute but also that the temple of the great goddess Artemis may be counted as nothing…"

Acts 19:27 · ESV

? Where do I stay calm until something costs me — and then react instantly?
What am I really protecting in that moment?

— Father, make me one who chooses truth even when it costs me.

03
DAY 03 A Scream, Not a Song Wednesday

Endless shouting becomes
a wall against the Word.

"Now some cried out one thing, some another, for the assembly was in confusion, and most of them did not know why they had come together."

Acts 19:32 · ESV

? When God's Word presses on a sore spot, what words, busyness, or noise do I use to drown it out?

— Lord, quiet my voice so I can hear yours.

04
DAY 04 Is My Jesus the Real One? Thursday

Is the Jesus I hold
a projection of my desires?

"…this Paul has persuaded and turned away a great many people, saying that gods made with hands are not gods."

Acts 19:26 · ESV

? Has Jesus become little more than a "good connection" who solves my daily problems?
Who is the crucified and risen Lord to me?

— Father, let me love the Jesus of Scripture, not one I have made.

05
DAY 05 The String That Never Breaks Friday

It didn't snap —
it was never the string.

"I am the vine; you are the branches. Whoever abides in me and I in him, he it is that bears much fruit, for apart from me you can do nothing."

John 15:5 · ESV

? Money, career, degrees, connections — what have I been holding as my "string"?
This week, what will I let go of, and whom will I hold instead?

— Lord, let me cling to you alone, the string that never breaks.

This Week's Takeaway

Honesty · Discernment · Surrender

Not a louder voice, but greater courage.

Closing Prayer

Lord,
frenzy makes us shout, but courage brings us to our knees.
Let us hold fast to Jesus, the string that never breaks, and rise with you.

— AMEN.
