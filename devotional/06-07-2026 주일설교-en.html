Acts 9:19b–31 · Saul's Conversion and the Decade in Tarsus

When no one
is looking for you,

wasted?

Even Saul, transformed from persecutor to apostle, had a forgotten decade — years over which Scripture falls silent.

DAY 01 · Zeal Can Be an Idol

Burning zeal,
aimed the
wrong way.

Saul believed he lived for God more than anyone — yet he was intoxicated with his own righteousness.

"And I was advancing in Judaism beyond many of my own age among my people, so extremely zealous was I for the traditions of my fathers."

Galatians 1:14 · ESV

DAY 01 · Meditation

Self-worship in the name of religion
is the most cleverly disguised idolatry.

We can worship God and still be practicing idolatry. If we believe in Jesus merely as a ticket to heaven, or as a means to a more comfortable life, then our desires are no different from the world's.

That was Saul before his conversion. He thought himself pure and passionate, yet the sword he wielded was piercing the Lord of life himself. When zeal points the wrong way, the zeal itself becomes sin.

DAY 01 · Reflect + Pray

Re-examine the direction of your faith.

01 What am I really believing in Jesus for? Is there a hidden desire of my own buried in that motive?
02 Among the things I call "zeal for God," is there anything that is, in truth, for myself?

Lord, let me see honestly where my zeal is aimed.
Make my faith a life for you, not a faith for myself.

Amen.
DAY 02 · Immediately, He Proclaimed

Once you have met him,
you cannot
stay silent.

Having met the risen Lord on the Damascus road, Saul proclaimed at once — knowing exactly what it would cost.

"And immediately he proclaimed Jesus in the synagogues, saying, 'He is the Son of God.'"

Acts 9:20 · ESV

DAY 02 · Meditation

Whoever has met the risen Lord
can never live as before.

Saul surely knew what his change of allegiance would unleash. Yesterday's ally would become today's enemy; everything he had built would be lost.

And yet he proclaimed "immediately." No — he could not do otherwise. For the one who has met the risen Lord, silence is no longer an option.

At
once.

— For him, there was no reason to wait.

DAY 02 · Reflect + Pray

Examine the silence of your lips.

01 Is there a place where I have failed to speak the gospel — afraid of the cost, or waiting for a "better time"?
02 If I have truly met the risen Lord in person, what should be different about today?

Lord, let my encounter with you become real change.
Give me lips that proclaim you boldly, whatever the cost.

Amen.
DAY 03 · The One Who Vouched

One person's word
opened a closed door.

When no one would believe Saul, Barnabas staked his own name to vouch for him.

"But Barnabas took him and brought him to the apostles and declared to them how on the road he had seen the Lord… and how he had preached boldly in the name of Jesus."

Acts 9:27 · ESV

DAY 03 · Meditation

God looks less at what we achieved,
and more at how we ran the race.

The disciples in Jerusalem could not believe Saul's conversion. Three years had not erased the memory of the man who led the charge in killing Stephen.

Then Barnabas, the "son of encouragement," stepped forward. A trusted man staked his own name to vouch for Saul. God asks not whom we outran, but whom we walked beside.

DAY 03 · Reflect + Pray

Become someone's Barnabas.

01 Is there someone in the church I find hard to believe in — someone I am quietly pushing away?
02 Who is the one person I could lift up by staking my own trust and name as a bridge for them?

Lord, let vouching come before judging in me.
Use me as a bridge of encouragement to mend broken bonds.

Amen.
DAY 04 · Forgotten, Yet Prepared

A silent decade
upheld twenty
years of ministry.

Over the decade in Tarsus, where Saul returned in disgrace, Scripture is silent — yet God was at work.

DAY 04 · Meditation

"And when the brothers learned this, they brought him down to Caesarea and sent him off to Tarsus."

Acts 9:30 · ESV

Saul returned in disgrace to the hometown that had expected a hero. And there he spent more than ten years — years over which Scripture is utterly silent.

Yet in that forgotten time God was ripening Saul's theology. The place that looked like a dead stop was in fact the base camp that would carry the rest of his ministry.

DAY 04 · Reflect + Pray

Reinterpret your wilderness season.

01 Where is my "Tarsus" right now — a season that feels stalled, hidden, or forgotten?
02 If I read that season by the Father's word rather than the world's eyes, what changes?

Lord, let me read forgotten time as prepared time.
Help me trust your faithful shaping, even where it is unseen.

Amen.
DAY 05 · Light Through Weakness

Light spills out
through the cracks.

The less we hold, the more gloriously Christ is revealed through our weakness.

"My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness… I will boast all the more gladly of my weaknesses, so that the power of Christ may rest upon me."

2 Corinthians 12:9 · ESV

Direction · Waiting · Weakness

The three things that shaped Saul — and the same hand shaping us today.

DAY 05 · Closing Meditation + Prayer

Take one thing
from this week's reflection.

The cardboard box shone with light only after it was crushed. A seed's true power is released only when the seed dies. The world relentlessly attacks our weak places, but God works through those very weaknesses — to save me, to save you, to save the world.

01 What weakness does the world attack in me? How might that very place become where light spills out?
02 Like the transformed Paul, will you resolve to sing of the living Jesus with the whole of your life?

Lord, let your power be revealed through my weakness.
Make me one who sings of the living Jesus with my whole life.

Amen.
