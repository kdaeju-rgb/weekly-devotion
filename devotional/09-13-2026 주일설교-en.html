DAY 01 · The World's True Face

This is simply
what the world is.

When Paul preached, he was met with mockery. The world has always responded this way.

"Now when they heard of the resurrection of the dead, some mocked. But others said, 'We will hear you again about this.'"

Acts 17:32 · ESV

DAY 01 · Reflection

The fairness we cling to
was always about ourselves.

Today's young adults cry out for fairness, but their fairness too is fairness only when it benefits them, and unfairness the moment it costs them. Meritocracy and constitutional rights alike became self-centered justice.

The world has no power to deliver the justice we long for. Just as Paul was mocked in Athens, the world will always find those who proclaim the true God strange — and laugh at them.

DAY 01 · Application + Prayer

Examine where you looked this week.

01 When did the world disappoint you this week?
02 Where did you turn for comfort and answers in that moment?

Lord, let my hope rest in you, not in this world.
Let me seek fairness and justice in you alone.

— Amen.
DAY 02 · Do Not Be Afraid

Do not be afraid,
but speak.

Corinth was a desperate place for the gospel. Yet the Lord came to Paul in a vision.

"And the Lord said to Paul in the night by a vision, 'Do not be afraid, but go on speaking and do not be silent, for I am with you, and no one will attack you to harm you, for I have many in this city who are my people.'"

Acts 18:9-10 · ESV

DAY 02 · Reflection

Resolve does not mean
fear disappears.

Paul had resolved to suffer for the Lord, but resolve did not remove his fear. To this very Paul, the Lord came in a vision and said, do not be silent, but speak.

Strengthened, Paul stayed a year and six months teaching the word, and a meaningful movement began — Crispus the synagogue ruler and Sosthenes came to faith.

DAY 02 · Application + Prayer

Take one step instead of fear.

01 Where am I staying silent right now out of fear?
02 What courage does the promise of his presence give me today?

Lord, I believe you are present where I am afraid.
Let me take one step today instead of staying silent.

— Amen.
DAY 03 · The World's Tribunal

As long as it costs
them nothing.

Sosthenes, the synagogue ruler, was beaten before the tribunal — and Gallio looked away.

"And they all seized Sosthenes, the ruler of the synagogue, and beat him in front of the tribunal. But Gallio paid no attention to any of this."

Acts 18:17 · ESV

DAY 03 · Reflection

This was Roman law—
this is the world's law.

Gallio judged with plain common sense that the charge was not a matter for Roman law. Yet in the face of violence, he stayed silent. Not intervening unless it threatens you — that is the law of this world.

The standard of fairness and justice we hold is no different. Fine when it benefits us, intolerable when it inconveniences us — we too may be Roman citizens living by Roman law.

DAY 03 · Application + Prayer

Be a neighbor, not a bystander.

01 Is there someone I overlooked simply because it cost me nothing to do so?
02 What is my attitude toward the vulnerable people around me?

Lord, make me a neighbor, not a bystander.
Let me love even when it brings me no benefit.

— Amen.
DAY 04 · Trade and Gospel

Making tents
and preaching the word.

Paul stayed with Priscilla and Aquila, making tents while teaching the word for a year and six months.

"and because he was of the same trade he stayed with them and worked, for they were tentmakers by trade."

Acts 18:3 · ESV

DAY 04 · Reflection

Inside and outside the church
are never separate.

Paul did not live in the synagogue twenty-four hours a day. He never separated making tents from preaching the word. School and faith, work and the Christian life are no different.

As we walk out the church doors, we must keep asking: "What words, what expression, what choices will I make today before a holy God?" That question is what it means to be filled with the Spirit.

DAY 04 · Application + Prayer

Examine yourself at work today.

01 At work today, what did my words and expressions actually communicate?
02 Have I noticed someone sitting alone, at church or at work?

Lord, let my life inside and outside the church be the same.
Let my trade itself become a place where your word is preached.

— Amen.
DAY 05 · Everyday Faithfulness

Not a dramatic
experience.

What was missing was not zeal, but everyday faithfulness.

"And he stayed a year and six months, teaching the word of God among them."

Acts 18:11 · ESV

DAY 05 · Reflection

Loving without reward
cannot be counted in numbers.

Hours of prayer and chapters of Scripture read can be counted, but loving without reward, praying without answers, and helping someone we dislike is never dramatic. So we keep mistaking visible religious activity for the whole of faith.

If we, like Paul, live faithfully in the ordinary for just a year and six months, we will see, even in barren ground, a changed self — and people changed through us.

A Closing Prayer

Take one thing
from this week's devotional.

01 What ordinary place is God calling me to be faithful in this week?
02 What one sentence from this week will you carry into daily life?

Lord, let me live today in a way worthy of Christ.
Let me live boldly, faithfully, and steadily.

— Amen.
